Flashback Repository opens today. Same rule as always: own the original, then enjoy the cut.

For the launch we’re also putting out **three new fan edits** timed for Halloween — a linked set by staff and style, not just by monster posters:

- [*The Mummy (1999) — Debugged*](/the-mummy-1999-debugged)
- [*The Mummy Returns (2001) — Debugged*](/the-mummy-returns-2001-debugged)
- [*Van Helsing (2004) — DeAnna’d*](/van-helsing-deanna-d)

These pictures share adventure DNA: **Stephen Sommers**, **Alan Silvestri**, **Bob Ducsay**, and that Universal creature-feature swagger (with faces that hop the trilogy, like Kevin J. O’Connor). Watch them back-to-back and the family resemblance is obvious.

What also matched — in the wrong way — is how badly each theatrical cut can feel **compromised in the edit**. *Van Helsing* is the loud case: great flair, constantly stabbed by choppy pacing and cringe takes that should never have survived. The *Mummy* movies are the quieter tragedy. The first two are warm, funny, and sturdy; later hands (and a lot of internet “fixing”) seem almost **hostile to those originals** — as if the job were to apologize for them instead of protect them. These Debugged / DeAnna’d cuts are the opposite impulse: keep the adventure, drop the damage, and let the movies breathe again.

Happy Halloween. Own your discs, then press Play.
