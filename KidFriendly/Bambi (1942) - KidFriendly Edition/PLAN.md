# Editorial plan: *Bambi* (1942) — KidFriendly Edition

Companion to [README.md](README.md) and [remove-bambi-kidfriendly-ranges.json](remove-bambi-kidfriendly-ranges.json).

## Goals (age ~3)

| Goal | Success |
|------|---------|
| No Man scare | First meadow stampede, gunshot panic, "Man was in the forest" removed; join to snow |
| No mother's death | Thicket hunt, "Mother!" search, Father "can't be with you" removed; join to spring song |
| No Ronno violence | Antler fight cut; join to love song |
| No fire / dogs / climax | Second Man through wounded Bambi removed; twins-only ending |
| Mild winter | Keep snow + Thumper ice play; cut hunger and long-winter sadness |
| Keep joy & romance | Birth, friends, bird/Flower, meadow, twitterpated, Faline, love song, twin fawns |

## Removal blocks (source timeline, seconds — before pad; see JSON for final)

| Block | ~Time | Content |
|-------|--------|---------|
| 1 | 1830–1953 | Meadow panic through "Man was in the forest" (extend in QC if pheasant earlier) |

| 3 | 3233–3340 | Ronno fight (mostly silent — QC in Kdenlive) |
