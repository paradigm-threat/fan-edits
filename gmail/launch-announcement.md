### What Flashback Repository is

A personal home for my **movie remixes** — theatrical films I own, re-edited for taste and clarity. Not a pirate archive, not a studio, not a replacement for buying the movie. Each title page explains what changed.

I built it because publishing this kind of work on Reddit, FanEdit.org, or the usual fan-edit venues has been **unfeasible** for me: posts get banned almost immediately, even when I own the original and try to follow the rules. So the library lives here instead, under a simple rule: **own the commercial original, then enjoy the cut.**

More on why: [Why we’re doing this](https://flashbackrepository.org/motivation)

### What I’m usually fixing

Often several at once:

- **Cringe** and tone-breakers that yank you out of the movie  
- **Needless gore and violence** that isn’t load-bearing for the story  
- **Plot holes** and muddy storytelling  
- **Obnoxious edit / post-production choices** that hurt films that still had a great core — *Van Helsing* is the loud example; *Final Fantasy: The Spirits Within* is another (a picture that never got the international Japanese-track release many of us wanted)

The Halloween trio above is that impulse applied to three creature features that share a family resemblance — and that each feel compromised, in different ways, in the theatrical cut.

### Submissions

If you’ve made a movie edit and want it considered: you can send a finished export as a **reference**, but I **prefer to re-render from source**.

The standard here is **Kdenlive** (`.kdenlive`) + **melt** (MLT). That keeps every remix editable with open-source tools — open the project, tweak the cut, re-render. You mainly need a decent CPU; no proprietary NLE required.

Details: [Submissions](https://flashbackrepository.org/submit) · GitLab: https://gitlab.com/clevertree/fan-edits
