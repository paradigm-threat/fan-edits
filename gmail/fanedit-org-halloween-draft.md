## Shared block (paste into both threads)

**Why I fanedit**

I own these films. I like the cores. I don’t always like the theatrical cut. FanFixes, for me, are about taste and clarity: pull tone-breakers, needless creature-gore that isn’t load-bearing, muddy storytelling, and post-production choices that hurt a picture that still had a great engine underneath. Not a politics project — just wanting to watch the adventure again without flinching or fast-forwarding through the same beats every time.

When it fits, I also weave in video-game remix beds (credits or underscore) — that’s the other half of how I got here.

**Where this leaves release**

I’ve been a member here a long time. I’ve submitted for years and still don’t have access to the final / Faneditor submissions path (and I can’t open release threads in Fixes / Mixes). Elsewhere — Reddit, Discord, the usual fan-edit channels — I’m banned or shut out. Practically, there isn’t a community lane left for me to “release” these the normal way.

So I’m putting the library on my own site instead: **flashbackrepository.org**. Same Own-the-Source idea — own the commercial original, then watch the cut. I’m posting these ITW threads for anyone who still wants the cut lists and to talk craft. If you want a copy and you own the source, **PM** me; please don’t ask for links in-thread.
