Why this exists

This is not only about making better versions of movies. It is about the allowed part.

If you own a film, you ought to be able to improve your copy and share that cut with friends without treating the act itself as radioactive. Craft matters — cleaner pacing, less cruelty, a score that fits — but the deeper point is agency over media you already paid for.

What I am usually fixing:

  • Cringe and tone-breakers — jokes, beats, or performances that yank you out of a movie that otherwise works
  • Needless gore and violence — especially when it is not load-bearing for the story
  • Plot holes and muddy storytelling — trim or reshape so the spine is clearer
  • Obnoxious edit / post-production decisions — choppy pacing, survivor’s-guilt comedy, or “fixes” that feel hostile to a film’s original warmth
  • Missing or compromised releases — e.g. Final Fantasy: The Spirits Within never got the international Japanese-track release many of us wanted

Whenever it fits, cuts weave in video game remixes — not only in credits, but inside the film (Silent Hill, Sonic DeSchwartzed OC ReMixes, Castlevania beds in Van Helsing, and more).

Why I had to launch my own site

Existing fanedit communities set useful ownership norms. They also feel deeply compromised — a permitted sandbox that never approaches the full potential of owned-media remix. Quality and dissent get filtered. Serious posts can be banned almost immediately even when the editor owns the original and follows posted rules.

That is not abstract for me. I am permanently banned from /r/fanedits and effectively shut out of the usual FanEdit.org / Discord / Reddit channels for this work.

What happened, in short:

  • Within about two days of posting my edits, I was permanently banned. They would not let me appeal.
  • The mod note claimed I was banned for “using pirated sources.” Nobody asked me for a copy. Nobody checked. I can prove my sources were not pirated — I even discuss buying legitimate discs (including the Spiritual Edition DVD from eBay, which is allowed).
  • Before that, my Sonic Trilogy DeSchwartzed post was removed under “Political / Religious Topics” after hecklers piled on. I asked them to review the post (there was no political/religious commentary in the OP). They refused a clean repost, muted me from mod mail, then banned me on the piracy accusation.

So the venues that claim to exist so people who own the movie can share taste-driven cuts are not open to me — even when I follow the ownership rule they advertise. I do not think that is an accident. As Hollywood gets worse, the last thing industry-aligned gatekeeping wants is a scene where owned remixes compete on quality. Controlled opposition looks like a friendly forum that never lets serious work stand out.

Flashback Repository is the outlet I had to build myself. Make the cut, document it, host it (site + Drive + primarily IPFS), require that viewers own the commercial original. No pirate archive. No apology for wanting better versions of films we already bought.
