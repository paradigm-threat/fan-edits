# The Pentagon Papers (2003) — Widescreen

Sharper **16:9** presentation of the FX / Paramount TV movie. No story cuts — only a cleaner frame for modern screens.

A second English track cites the McNaughton / AU-058 investigation and the fact that this film **was not allowed in theaters** — FX TV only, and it never saw an HD release.

## Why this cut

The commercial disc is a **4:3** master framed for standard-def television. Softness is DVD-era TV, not a missing theatrical negative. That is also the investigation beat: the film **was not allowed a theatrical run**, and it **never saw an HD release**. Short cards for that live on the **English (citations)** track only.

## What changed

- Square-pixel restore, then upscale and center-crop to **16:9** (not stretched, not outpainted)
- Default **English** — dialogue only
- Optional **English (citations)** — McNaughton, Tonkin/Hanyok, TV-only / no HD (never on the default track)

## Subtitles

Your player may offer two English tracks:

- **English** — dialogue only (default)
- **English (citations)** — dialogue plus short on-screen notes
