## L3 — editor / AU-062 (credits + rare `[Edit:]`)

- The *name* “Mountain of Heaven” is suppressed, not unknown. The picture is common in Egyptian signs and in this open — not a cult in-joke.
- Same silhouette as *The Big Lebowski* Gutterballs. Coens’ official reasons: dreams mash leftovers, Busby Berkeley. Neither is the payload. Payload: shared sky-religion, including the Asgard–Viking lock — public, not a phallic gag.
- That public-ness is why AU-062 calls the **opening** NSA-aligned (whole-film NSA is **not** claimed; agency direction unproven).
- Two Arab types: Cairo hangman vs Ardeth/Magi as honorable world-security “warrior for God.” Accords / Isaac-first / NSA-pact readings are **not in the script** — see PT war dossiers.
- **2026-09-24:** *The Mummy Returns* is a **separate NSA title lock** (twelve tribes / no bad Arabs). This film still has the hangman type. Credits updated.

## Why scenes are removed

- **Usually violence** — scarabs that kill or eat people (Hom-Dai pour, swarm, Burns).
- **Sometimes unrealism** — a brief gore beat still comes out if the **next shot erases the injury**.

**Jonathan arm (source ~01:36:33–01:37:33):** a scarab burrows under the skin; a dirty hunting knife opens a large chunk of arm; the next shot he is unhurt — no blood, bandage, or scar. Medically impossible. Cut as a continuity reset, not a recovery.
