# Editorial plan: *The Mummy* (1999) — Debugged

Companion to [README.md](README.md), [INVESTIGATION.md](INVESTIGATION.md), and [remove-mummy-debugged-ranges.json](remove-mummy-debugged-ranges.json).

**Target:** kid-friendlier adventure cut. Removals are **usually violence** (scarabs that kill or eat people). **Sometimes unrealism** — if the next shot erases a serious injury.

**Source:** 4K YTS rip + `subs/The Mummy Debugged.srt` (YTS 119140, source times). Output dialogue is `The Mummy Debugged.kdenlive.srt` / `subs/The Mummy Debugged.english.srt`.

## Why cuts exist

| Reason | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Violence** | Live beetles eating people, swarm close-ups, Burns finished with scarabs. |
| **Unrealism** | Jonathan’s arm is opened with a dirty knife after a scarab burrows under the skin; the next shot he is unhurt. |

Gunfights, Imhotep, and the Magi stay.
