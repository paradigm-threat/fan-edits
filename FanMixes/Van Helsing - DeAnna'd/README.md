# Van Helsing (2004) — DeAnna'd

Cut that removes **Anna** as much as possible without breaking the story, and strips as many cringe beats as we can — especially around the Frankenstein monster. Some score beds are swapped for *Castlevania: Symphony of the Night* cues.

## Why this cut

The movie has real flair and color — the same Universal adventure DNA as *The Mummy* (1999) / *The Mummy Returns* (2001) (Stephen Sommers, Alan Silvestri, Bob Ducsay). What hurts it is the **editing**: choppy, excessive, lined with cringey lines that kill the action.

Contemporary reviews said as much ([Salon](https://www.salon.com/2004/05/07/van_helsing/), [Slate](https://slate.com/culture/2004/05/van-helsing-cursed.html), [EW](https://ew.com/article/2004/05/05/van-helsing-2/)). This edit exists because the movie is worth saving.

**DeAnna:** keep only what the story still needs from Anna. **Cringe:** cut tone-breaking comedy and awkward monster beats when the plot can stand without them. **Flow:** restore action continuity the theatrical cut keeps interrupting.

## What changed

- Anna reduced to story-essential beats
- Tone-breaking comedy and awkward Frankenstein-monster beats cut when possible
- Ice-block Frankenstein travel gag stays out (intentional)
- Werewolf-cure explanation restored so the midnight deadline makes sense

## Score note

Fans often connect Symphony of the Night’s **“Dracula's Castle”** with Alan Silvestri’s Transylvania cues. Unverified, but the shared four-note idea shows up more than once. Short on-screen notes for this live on the **English (citations)** subtitle track.
