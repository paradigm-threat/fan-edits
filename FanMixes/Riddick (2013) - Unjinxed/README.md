# Riddick (2013) — Unjinxed

A dialogue-trimmed *Riddick* that keeps the Unjinxed tone, front-loads the Necromonger “wrong planet” flashback as a linear prologue, and drops the opening predator beat so the desert survival story starts clean.

## Why this cut

Theatrical *Riddick* dumps you into desert survival and only later explains why he’s stranded. This cut puts that exposition up front, without mid-film flashbacks, while keeping the Unjinxed dialogue trims and the Necromonger epilogue at the end.

## What changed

- Distracting, sexist, and exaggerated dialogue trimmed throughout
- Opening vulture / pterosaur kill removed; desert story starts clean
- Vaako / Furya-bait conspiracy block stays cut
- Necromonger / wrong-planet flashback plays **before** the desert survival plot (prologue); in-body flashback omitted from the main timeline
- Savanna bridge kept once after main survival; Necromonger epilogue remains at the **end**
