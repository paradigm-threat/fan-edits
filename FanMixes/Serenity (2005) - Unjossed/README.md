# Serenity (2005) — Unjossed Edition

A family/kids cut of *Serenity*: strict on Reaver gore, keeps the action and core plot, and leaves favorite Whedon beats (including “leaf on the wind” and the final fight) alone unless they break tone after horror.

## Why this cut

For an audience that wants the Firefly movie without Reaver shock cuts. Liked one-liners stay unless they break tone after horror (the batteries beat is the main dialog cut). Linear theatrical flow — no reorder.

## What changed

- Opening: Reaver attack gore trimmed
- Whole Kaylee/Jayne “nethers / batteries” beat removed
- Haven massacre shortened to Mal’s reaction — bodies on posts / gore out
- Maidenhead: reaver-gore inserts only; martial arts fight kept
- Lab: reaver specimen / gore visuals trimmed
- Mr. Universe reaver horror reduced
- Reaver fleet / corpse shock beats trimmed
- “Leaf on the wind” and final Operative / Serenity fight **kept**
