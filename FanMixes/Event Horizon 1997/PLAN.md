# Cut inventory: *Event Horizon* (1997)

Companion to [README.md](README.md). Theatrical cut. Timecodes TBD against our rip once razor work starts.

The disclosure (gravity drive, rotating magnetic field, NSA, portal = hell) stays. Everything below is on the chopping block or under review because it either **offends people out of the movie** or **pads Earth psych drama that never pays off**.

---

## 1. Earth-backstory psych padding (no plot reason)

These are the lame “personal demons from back home” beats. The ship does not need them. The portal does not need them. They are Hollywood guilt-furniture bolted onto a technology/horror spine.

| Sequence | Whose Earth problem | Why it fails |
|----------|---------------------|--------------|
| **Miller’s burning man** | Prior rescue: he left a crewman in a fire | Repeated hallway / airlock visions. Used later to “motivate” his sacrifice. The mission already has stakes; this is soap guilt. |
| **Peters and her son** | Left her child on Earth; guilt | Hallucination of the boy luring her through the ship; mangled / bloody legs (theatrical still has the gore beat; longer maggot version was cut after tests). Pure maternal-guilt product. Zero bearing on the gravity drive. |
| **Weir and Claire (wife)** | Dead wife; suicide memory | Eyeless / sexualized Claire apparitions; bathroom / suicide flash. Turns the designer’s arc into a haunted-husband melodrama. The ship already owns him once he explains the drive. |
| **DJ’s scar / vivisection fear** | Childhood surgery gone wrong (mostly cut; fear still telegraphed) | Exists so his later gutting “fulfills” a personal phobia. Cheap symmetry. |
| **Justin “fascinated by the Horizon” backstory** | Mostly cut from theatrical | Was meant to “explain” why he walks into the core. Curiosity + bad luck is enough. |
| **Starck ↔ Miller romance hint** | Cut from theatrical | Would have been more Earth/crew soap on top of the rescue. |

**Edit posture:** Strip or gut these vision loops. Keep only what is required so a character still dies or acts when the plot needs it — without the Earth soap lecture.

## 2. Notorious gore / sexual violence (people refuse the film)

Paramount already gutted the rough cut after test screenings. What remains is still enough to make people walk out — and that kills the disclosure.

| Sequence | What it is | Why people reject it |
|----------|------------|----------------------|
| **“Bloody orgy” / original-crew hell tape** | Found-footage of the first crew in the gateway: rape, cannibalism, eye-gouging, self-disembowelment, pipes, spikes | The signature offense. Theatrical is the truncated “Visions from Hell.” Still the reason many will never finish the movie. |
| **Miller vs Weir — extended hell inserts** | More torture flashes during the final fight | Same tape-logic, second helping. |
| **Peters’ son — mangled legs** | Child with ruined legs / blood (maggots were worse; mostly cut) | Child mutilation as scare. Offensive and pointless. |
| **Claire suicide flash (graphic)** | Weir’s wife’s death, bloodier in rough cut; theatrical still ugly | Suicide + body horror as character wallpaper. |
| **Weir regenerated / eyeless Weir** | Self-enucleation; blood-filled cryo; return from the ship | Body-horror money shots after the point is already made. |
| **DJ hung and opened** | Miller finds DJ vivisected, guts displayed | Torture-porn payoff for the “scar fear” pad. |
| **Peters’ fall / corpse** | Death after the son lure; lingering on the body | Gore punctuation on a guilt plot that should not exist. |
| **Justin in the core / gateway** | Pulled into the singularity; catatonic aftermath | Keep a short version if the plot needs “the drive is lethal”; cut the lingering torment tourism. |
| **Airlock / explosive decompression beats** | Smith / others; body destruction | Often kept for plot; trim gratuitous lingering. |
| **Sexual assault energy (Weir → Starck)** | Menace / attack framing in the late ship | Crosses from spooky to punitive sexual threat. Out of scope for a disclosure cut. |

## 3. Hollywood vacuum-shred / “space is empty” (physics propaganda)

Same family as *Alien Resurrection* airlock body-horror. Not “edgy sci-fi.” A **repeated demo** that space vacuum tears people apart — which is false — and that trains the public to believe there is **nothing** between planets and **no atmosphere** on the Moon.

| Sequence | Problem |
|----------|---------|
| Airlock / hatch opens → bodies shredded / explosive boil | Classic trope. Bodies do not explode in vacuum ([NASA Ask an Astrophysicist](https://imagine.gsfc.nasa.gov/ask_astro/answers/970603.html)). |
| Any beat that sells interplanetary space as absolute empty “nothing” | Misdirection. Space is a **plasma medium** (solar wind, current sheets) — [Electric Universe](https://www.electricuniverse.info/); [Holoscience — Voyager / electrical environment](https://www.holoscience.com/wp/voyager-probes-the-suns-electrical-environment/); [Thunderbolts](https://www.thunderbolts.info/); [SAFIRE](https://www.safireproject.com/). |
| Moon-as-total-vacuum grammar (when the film or franchise rhetoric leans on it) | Absolute “no atmosphere” is the brand. Measurements still show a lunar **exosphere** and solar-wind plasma ([NASA — Moon’s atmosphere](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/lunar-atmosphere/); [NASA — Solar wind on the Moon](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/solar-wind/)). |

**Edit posture:** Cut the vacuum-tear spectacle. Do not keep a fake physics sermon. The gravity drive stays; the empty-space myth does not.

## 5. What we are *not* cutting for offense

- Gravity drive briefing (**NSA** “code-black,” **rotating magnetic field**, singularity fold).
- First activation / disappearance lore.
- Neptune recovery / Lewis & Clark arrival.
- Core geometry, the gateway as hell (concept), ship-as-trap.
- Opening shot (space → debris past camera → planet → ship) — *Metroid Prime* handshake.

---

## 6. Working rule

If a beat exists only to:

1. punish a character with **Earth guilt**, or  
2. show **torture / rape / child mutilation / suicide porn**, or  
3. stall for jump scares, or  
4. demo the **Hollywood vacuum-shred / empty-space** myth,

it goes. If a beat teaches the **drive**, the **portal**, or the **cost of using it**, it stays in the shortest honest form.
