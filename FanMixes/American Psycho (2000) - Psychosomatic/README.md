# American Psycho (2000) — Psychosomatic

A deshocked *American Psycho*: pull **shock sex** and **on-screen murder
spectacle** so the film stays a creepy satire/horror of implication — you feel
the dread without watching the kills. Slogan: **Creepy enough. No snuff reel.**

## Motivation

Theatrical Bateman already works as psychological horror: business-card
peacocking, detective visits, a confession nobody believes. The film does not
need shock-sex padding or graphic murder set pieces. This cut aims to leave the
yuppie nightmare intact and refuse the snuff-adjacent spectacle.

About **13 minutes** across six picture holes are out. Labels below were
re-derived from the **current** `playlist4` holes + attached
`*.kdenlive.srt` (Remaster source wired; sync checked in-editor).

## Changes

- **Spa blood-lust / “mask of sanity” VO + office Christmas** out (~00:20–00:22).
  Opening morning-routine lotion stays.
- **~7s after packing Allen’s bag** (~00:28) — **TODO (picture):** likely
  disposal/gore residual before the answering machine.

- **Long mid-film razor** (~00:39–00:46) joins Christie “Armani tie…” to club
  “You think I’m dumb…”. Silent bridges TBD (possible shock-sex). Much of what

  Gorbachev / “murders and executions” setup**) looks like **collateral** —

- **Allen apartment realtor + Jean / “Just say no!”** out (~01:13–01:16).
- **Mid–Carnes “I chopped Allen’s fucking head off”** blurt trimmed (~01:18).
