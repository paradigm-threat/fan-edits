# American Psycho (2000) — Psychosomatic

A deshocked *American Psycho*: pull **shock sex** and **on-screen murder
spectacle** so the film stays a creepy satire/horror of implication — you feel
the dread without watching the kills. Slogan: **Creepy enough. No snuff reel.**

## Motivation

Theatrical (and Uncut) Bateman already works as psychological horror: the
business-card peacocking, the detective visits, the confession that nobody
believes. What the movie does not need is the **shock-sex** padding or the
**graphic murder** set pieces. Those play as gore/porn punctuation. This cut
aims to leave the yuppie nightmare intact and refuse the snuff-adjacent
spectacle — enough of a creepy horror movie without having to actually see the
murders happen.

About **13 minutes** across six picture holes are out. Scene labels below are
from the source-timed SRT overlapping those holes (**probable, not
picture-proven**). Hole *times* are certain from Kdenlive. Remaster 1080p SRT
is in `subs/` for sync reference (Uncut 4K YTS source still missing on disk).

## Changes

- **Blood-lust / “mask of sanity” spa VO** out (~00:20) — attendant “beautiful
  skin” into the frenzy VO (*not* the opening morning-routine lotion, which stays).
- **Office Christmas** swept in the same hole (~00:21–00:22) — mistletoe /
  Snowball / Nell’s. *(May be collateral — confirm on picture.)*
- **~7s silent after packing Allen’s bag** (~00:28) — **TODO (picture):** likely
  body-disposal / cleanup residual between “London” and the answering machine.
- **“Head on a stick”** punchline trimmed (~00:37).
- **Long mid-film block** out (~00:39–00:46) — Christie exit, **Uncut
  threesome / shock-sex window** (~00:42), Courtney, AIDS-table rant, bathroom
  coke; resumes at “where do you work out?” Second Kimball follow-up sits in

- **Allen apartment realtor + Jean / “Just say no!”** out (~01:13–01:16).
- **Mid–Carnes “I chopped Allen’s fucking head off”** blurt trimmed (~01:18).
