# Star Trek: First Contact (1996) — No Ahab

A bridge-focused cut that skips Picard’s Captain Ahab / Moby-Dick meltdown and the stay-and-fight spiral so the Borg crisis stays tactical.

## Motivation

After the bridge reports they are overrun and “shooting blanks,” theatrical Picard digs into revenge fantasy and literature instead of commanding the evacuate. That Ahab beat — and the later soft apology to Worf — pulls *First Contact* into wounded-ego drama the rest of the film does not need. This edit jumps to calm **“Prepare to evacuate,”** keeps auto-destruct / Phoenix, and lets the Borg story move without the meltdown.

## Changes

- After the overrun / “shooting blanks” report, cuts straight to Picard’s “Prepare to evacuate” (short wipe).
- Removes the stay-and-fight argument and Worf’s Aye/Wait proposal.
- Removes the Captain Ahab / Moby-Dick meltdown.
- Removes the later regret / “bravest man” apology to Worf.
- Continues with auto-destruct / Phoenix; alphabet joke cuts to “See you on Gravett Island.”
