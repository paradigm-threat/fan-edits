# Contact (1997) — No Alphas

Directed by **Robert Zemeckis**. A cut of *Contact* that strips obnoxious alpha-male private beats so Ellie’s story is the signal, the machine, and the cosmos — not their insecurities.

Same **1997** shelf in this repo as [Event Horizon](../Event%20Horizon%201997/) (**Paul W.S. Anderson** — not PTA) and [Boogie Nights — The Dream](../Boogie%20Nights%20%281997%29%20-%20The%20Dream/) (**Paul Thomas Anderson**). Three directors; one disclosure-era year. The two Pauls were billed so alike that the public still merges them — Hollywood confusion as control. We name them here so that fog does not win.

## Motivation

Theatrical *Contact* spends a lot of runtime on Palmer Joss’s private pressure and Drumlin’s smarm — insecure men competing for narrative space around Ellie. Hadden’s eccentric patronage is part of the myth; childhood Ted nostalgia and the CQ memorial are not required for the first-contact spine. This edit keeps **all Hadden scenes**, trims the alphas, fades the alien-shore Ted form on picture (audio kept), and leaves the schoolkids “awful waste of space” ending for viewers who know the film.

## Changes

- Removes or trims Palmer Joss private / pressure beats and Drumlin smarm.
- **All Hadden scenes stay.**
- Childhood Ted and the CQ memorial cut.
- Alien-shore Ted form: manual video fade (audio kept).
- Schoolkids “awful waste of space” ending kept.
