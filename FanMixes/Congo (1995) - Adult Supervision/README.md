# Congo (1995) — Adult Supervision

A leaner *Congo* that strips Dr. Peter Elliot’s childish / incel-adjacent berating of ex-CIA Karen Ross so the adventure can lead — and trims Travis/Captain Wanta screaming, some gore, plus a travel plot hole by moving the Bob Driscoll encounter forward.

## Motivation

Theatrical *Congo* frames Karen Ross as a competent field operator (ex-CIA / communications tech) who can fund the expedition, run the gear, and read a room. Peter Elliot spends long stretches on soft-boy status games — projected transactional analysis and flirtation-as-control she does not need while the jungle mission is on the line.

Signature beats that define the problem: after she saves the flight with cash, he digs in with “just because you came up with a wad of cash at the crucial moment…”; expertise insults (“geek with a cell phone,” circus dig); and using Amy as cover to call her ugly, then half-apologizing. Those incel moments are what this cut removes so Ross stays the adult in the room — **adult supervision** — and *Congo* plays as jungle adventure again.

Elliot’s irritating dialogue and the louder screaming beats wear out the adventure. Worse, the theatrical cut has the group meet Bob Driscoll nearly dead in the forest, then travel what looks like days across mountains to reach the place he staggered out of. Moving that encounter next to the campsite where he was attacked keeps geography honest (the original editors likely pushed the scene later to wake the audience during calmer stretches).

## Changes

- Removes much of Dr. Elliot’s incel-adjacent dialogue toward Karen Ross (wad-of-cash dig, expertise insults, ugly/cover-story beats, and similar soft-boy status games).
- Trims some screaming scenes by Travis and Captain Wanta.
- Cuts or reduces some gore.
- Moves the Bob Driscoll forest encounter forward so the group finds him right outside the campsite where he was attacked (instead of days of travel later).

### Still in cut (optional next pass)
