# Alien Resurrection (1997) — UnJossed

A leaner *Alien Resurrection* that strips out most of the gross, Joss Whedon–flavored body-horror gags while keeping the Betty crew’s escape intact.

## Motivation

The theatrical cut leans hard on Whedon’s quippy cruelty and hybrid-alien body horror. Those beats undercut the dread the franchise is better at — this edit dials that material back so the clone-Ripley story can play closer to an *Alien* film than a black comedy.

## Changes

- Removes most gross (Joss Whedon–inspired) sequences, including the birth and death of the hybrid alien.
