# Changes — Tron Legacy (2010) - The Miracle

Slogan: **Less Castor, more Bio-Digital Jazz, Man!**

Cut list from the Kdenlive timeline versus the original feature. A hole is a **source-time** window that is not on the video (or sparse audio) playlist.

**Timing note:** `Tron Legacy (2010) - The Miracle.kdenlive.srt` matches **source** (theatrical) times for the Castor/nightclub cuts — not the shortened edit’s clock. There is no `_sources/theatrical.srt`. Labels use that project SRT; holes with little or no subtitle text are flagged. Picture check still wins.

## Intent

Title **The Miracle** is about the movie’s AI message (utopia for everyone vs control and war) and Jeff Bridges’s ISO / “bio-digital jazz” speech. Cut Castor’s (Zuse’s) worst nightclub scenes (private lounge, deal with Clu / “End of line,” toast and disk grab), and trim about 11 minutes of slow padding, so that speech and the ISO story are easier to follow. Keep father/son, CLU’s takeover, Quorra as the last ISO, and Daft Punk / Grid action.

- Kdenlive: `Tron Legacy (2010) - The Miracle.kdenlive` (root = this project folder)
- Video sources: `_sources/` — BONE theatrical + *TRON: The Next Day* (gitignored; Kdenlive paths are `_sources/…`)
- Source-aligned SRT used for labels: `Tron Legacy (2010) - The Miracle.kdenlive.srt`
- Theatrical text SRT / OST mp3s: **missing** (BONE is PGS; Bubanee + 1982 Disney OST folders empty on NAS)
- Picture holes: **30** (≥2s; ~10.6 min of source removed)
