# Final Fantasy (2001) — The Phantom Menace

*Final Fantasy: The Spirits Within* — a minimalist re-cut that strips distracting acting and excess human-behavior beats, shortens gore, and restores a more Final Fantasy–like title placement — with the rare Japanese track and remix soundtrack upgrades.

## Why this cut

Much of the Baldwin and Buscemi acting pulls focus from the story Sakaguchi insisted on locking. This edit trims those beats, shortens gore, and puts the title where Final Fantasy stories usually land — after the intro, not in the first shot.

## What changed

- Distracting or poorly acted scenes removed
- Rare Japanese track as a second audio option
- Final Fantasy remix “A Temple of Chaos” by Tim Sheehy added
- Credits song *Spirit Dreams Inside* replaced with the Final Fantasy IV remix “Hymn to the Crystals” by RebeccaETripp
- Title card moved to after the intro sequence
