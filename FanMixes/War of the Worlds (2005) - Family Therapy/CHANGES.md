# Changes — War of the Worlds (2005) - Family Therapy

Cut list from the Kdenlive timeline versus the original feature. A hole is a source-time window that is not on the video (or sparse audio) playlist. Removed dialogue is taken from the original-timeline SRT that overlaps that window. If the scene is not obvious from the cues, it is marked **TBD**.

## Intent

Removes toxic family behavior so the film is about the invasion, not domestic abrasion. Also cuts Vincent/Julio solar-flare arguing, SpongeBob, some alien-death/lightning travel, and replaces Freeman’s intro with Richard Burton (addition/replacement, not a hole).

Later passes also cut more irritating dialog (Robbie/hill nagging, Rachel’s basement “No” loops), shorten disaster coverage so timing feels less padded (plane-wreckage tourism and ferry-capsize water beats), and drop the **second Sheryl / ferry-mob pass** while keeping the earlier crowd beat before that meet-cute.

**Source note:** This theatrical cut is a rather sloppy post-production job even compared with other Spielberg features — lots of simple continuity and coverage mistakes (repeated mob angles, dawdling disaster tourism, dialog that stalls under pressure). The FanMix has to correct those carefully, not only delete “content.”
