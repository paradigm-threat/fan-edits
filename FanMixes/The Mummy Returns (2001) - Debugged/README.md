# The Mummy Returns (2001) — Debugged

Kid-friendlier cut to match *The Mummy (1999) — Debugged*. Removes the **pygmy swarm** that kills the expedition (same “little creatures eating people” idea). Adventure, Anubis, and the finale stay.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Original** | The Mummy Returns (2001) |
| **Edit length** | about 1 hour 59 minutes (original ~2 hours 10 minutes) |
| **About** | Family-safer adventure cut |

## Why this cut

Same rule as the 1999 Debugged edit: no little creatures eating people on screen. The closest beat here is the **pygmy swarm**. The ending also trims **Izzy** (less of him late in the film, fewer of his lines).

## What changed

- First pygmy overrun of the camp removed
- Later pygmy chase / attack beat removed
- Ending: Izzy mostly cut from late beats; remaining lines reduced
- Credits / music beds include metal covers of *Zombies Ate My Neighbors* cues (see below)

## Extra music

Metal covers by **Larme Genie**, from *[Zombies Ate My Neighbors (The Metal Album)](https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mBWB5DWMPQXWO_uNHUJW4C47nFHuD97j00)* (2023). Original SNES score is Konami / *Zombies Ate My Neighbors*.

Cues used:

- *Pyramid of Fear (Metal Version)*
- *Zombie Panic (Metal Version)*
- *Evening of the Undead (Metal Version)*
