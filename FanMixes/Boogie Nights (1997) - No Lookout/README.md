# Boogie Nights (1997) — No Lookout

A single-block cut of PTA’s *Boogie Nights* that skips the late-film collapse from the office whip-pan through On the Lookout and the donut-shop stick-up, then resumes on the film’s own fade into the Rahad-planning scene.

## Why this cut

The On the Lookout / Valley night-drive / donut-shop kill stretch is a long bad-scene block that derails the movie’s late momentum and kills Buck for spectacle. Skipping it keeps Reed’s tape argument, accepts losing Amber’s collapse / custody / Buck’s death, and lands cleanly on the coke-deal planning beat.

## What changed

- Removes the stretch from Reed’s demo-tape office through Amber/Dirk, bank loan, custody, On the Lookout, the Valley night drive, and the donut-shop stick-up/kill
- Resumes on the film’s own black hold into the Rahad planning scene
